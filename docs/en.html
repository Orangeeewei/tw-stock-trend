Glossary (plain words)

Trust fund streak
"Trust funds" are Taiwan's domestic mutual funds. Their buys go through research and compliance, so several consecutive days of buying the same stock usually means homework was done — the most reliable smart-money signal in this market.
Foreign net buying
Foreign institutions bought more than they sold. Their positions are large and slow-moving; a turn to buying often marks the start of a medium-term trend.
Revenue YoY
Taiwan companies report revenue monthly (rare globally). Positive year-over-year growth is the simplest reliable fundamental signal.
Off 60-day high
How far the price sits below its 3-month high. Closer to 0% (or above) means it's nearing or breaking the prior high — the strongest setup; this strategy only takes names within -12%, skipping anything deep below the high.
Volume surge
5-day average volume vs the 20-day average. Above 1.2× means the sleeping stock is waking up.
Volume spike 🔥
Today's volume alone is 2×+ the 20-day average — big money is moving. Paired with a rising price, it often marks the start of a swing move.
Above 3 MAs
Close is above the 5-, 10- and 20-day moving averages at once: recent buyers are all in profit, so there's little overhead supply.
60-day moving average
The average cost of everyone who bought in the past three months. Index above it = most holders in profit; below = most are trapped and eager to sell.
Watchlist ⚠️
Flagged by the exchange for unusual trading (excessive moves or turnover). Not unbuyable, but clearly higher risk.
Disposition stocks
Stocks moved to managed matching (trades every 5–20 minutes) for repeated abnormal trading. Excluded from this report's candidates entirely.
Days on list
"New on list" means the signal just fired; many days on the list without a move is a reason for suspicion, not confidence.
Exit ladder (Wang's two-tier)
Instead of a fixed stop, exits follow the moving averages: trim half when the price breaks the 5-day MA, exit fully when it breaks the 10-day MA (the swing is over); the 10-day-low close is a third and final line. The prices under the Close column are these reference levels — not buy signals.
Unfilled downside gap ⚠️
The price gapped down and left a blank, un-traded zone that hasn't been filled back. Until it fills, that zone is overhead resistance and the trend isn't truly turning up.
False breakout ⚠️
It made a 60-day high (an apparent breakout) but the close fell back below the 5-day MA and the breakout neckline, with a downside gap after — the breakout may be fake, and chasing it risks getting trapped.
Institutional divergence ⚠️
The adjusted close made a fresh 60-day high — the strongest-looking setup — yet trust funds were net sellers over the past 5 days. Backtests show these divergent names are weaker (45.9% hit rate, ~2.94% below the index), so chasing without institutional support is risky.
