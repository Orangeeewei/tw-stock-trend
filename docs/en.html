Glossary (plain words)

Trust fund streak
"Trust funds" are Taiwan's domestic mutual funds. Their buys go through research and compliance, so several consecutive days of buying the same stock usually means homework was done — the most reliable smart-money signal in this market.
Foreign net buying
Foreign institutions bought more than they sold. Their positions are large and slow-moving; a turn to buying often marks the start of a medium-term trend.
Revenue YoY

60-day moving average
The average cost of everyone who bought in the past three months. Index above it = most holders in profit; below = most are trapped and eager to sell.

Exit ladder (Wang's two-tier)
Instead of a fixed stop, exits follow the moving averages: trim half when the price breaks the 5-day MA, exit fully when it breaks the 10-day MA (the swing is over); the 10-day-low close is a third and final line. The prices under the Close column are these reference levels — not buy signals.
Unfilled downside gap ⚠️
The price gapped down and left a blank, un-traded zone that hasn't been filled back. Until it fills, that zone is overhead resistance and the trend isn't truly turning up.
False breakout ⚠️
It made a 60-day high (an apparent breakout) but the close fell back below the 5-day MA and the breakout neckline, with a downside gap after — the breakout may be fake, and chasing it risks getting trapped.
